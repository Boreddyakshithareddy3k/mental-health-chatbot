# Safety & Ethics — Mental Health Chatbot

**Important:** This chatbot is a demo and NOT a replacement for medical help.

## Crisis Detection
If a message contains crisis language (self-harm, suicide, danger), the bot:
1. Stops normal conversation.
2. Shows an emergency response.
3. Provides helpline links.

## Emergency Helplines
- **India:** AASRA — +91 9820466726
- **USA:** 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — Dial 988
- **UK:** Samaritans — 116 123
- International list: https://www.opencounseling.com/suicide-hotlines

## No Diagnosis Policy
The bot does not give medical diagnoses.

## Privacy
Chats are not stored permanently; users can request deletion.
